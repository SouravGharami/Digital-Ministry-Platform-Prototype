About us

Faith was never meant to fit inside one building. We just gave it a wider door.

Digital Ministry Platform started with a simple observation: people were already looking for prayer, teaching, and community online — just scattered across timelines and algorithms that weren't built for it. We built a home for that search instead.

What we believe

The principles behind the Platform

Open access

Discovery should never be pay-to-rank

Every minister appears in search on the strength of their focus and fit for you — not the size of their ad budget.

Minister-first terms

Ministers set their own tiers and keep their community

We don't dictate pricing or perks. Each ministry shapes what support looks like for the people who follow them.

Every tradition, welcome

Built across denominations, not for one

Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox, Pentecostal, interfaith — the directory reflects the range of real faith communities.
